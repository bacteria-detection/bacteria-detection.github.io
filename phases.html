Phases of our work

Phase 1

Data Collection

Time-stamped microscopy of bacterial colonies under two backgrounds (plain and onion mixture) at five incubation timepoints — 2 h, 2.5 h, 3 h, 3.5 h, and 4 h, all captured at 60× magnification. Switch the species, then swipe or use the arrow keys to browse samples.

Phase 2

Annotation

A short walkthrough of our manual annotation workflow (built with a Python/Tkinter tool), followed by example annotation sessions for E. coli and Salmonella.

Annotation walkthrough — manual masking with the CVAT AI tool.
Phase 3

Data Preparation

Annotations are converted to COCO format — one polygon per colony, with category labels (E. coli / Salmonella) and per-instance bounding boxes. The interactive viewer below loads a sample COCO file and overlays each polygon directly on its source image.

What's in a sample

  • 4 sample slides: 2 E. coli (e2p_73, e4o_70) and 2 Salmonella (s2p_14, s4o_43)
  • 41 polygon annotations total across the four images
  • Per annotation: id, image_id, category_id, segmentation polygon, bbox, area, iscrowd
  • Resolution: 2160×1620 px at 60× magnification

Click a polygon or a row in Annotations in the viewer to see its COCO record.

Phase 4

Model Training

We are training and comparing about four models that span the cost/capacity spectrum — from a lightweight ResNetDS baseline through to heavyweight foundation models like SAMFPN and DINOFPN. The chart below shows the training and validation curves across the loss terms and the mean-IoU accuracy.

Inference
