Why it matters

Impact

Faster, more transparent bacterial detection translates directly into safer food and fewer outbreaks. Our work is built to be useful at the points where decisions are actually made.

⏱

Hours, not days

Traditional culture-based screening takes 5–7 days. Our deep-learning pipeline flags likely-positive samples within hours of imaging, so producers can act before product ships.

🛡

Safer food supply

Early E. coli and Salmonella detection helps prevent foodborne illness outbreaks and the recalls, hospitalizations, and economic losses that follow them.

🔍

Transparent decisions

Explainable AI (Grad-CAM, SHAP, LIME) shows why the model flagged a sample — a prerequisite for adoption in regulated food-safety workflows.

🌾

Real agricultural pipelines

Validated against onion-mixture backgrounds and multi-pathogen co-contamination — conditions that mirror actual produce-processing environments.

What's next

Sustainability & future use

Where this work is heading — and how it can keep delivering value while reducing the environmental footprint of routine food-safety testing.

01

Less reagent, less waste

Optical screening reduces dependence on disposable plates, media, and biochemical reagents per sample — shrinking the consumables footprint of routine testing.

02

More pathogens, same pipeline

The same time-stamped microscopy + segmentation approach extends naturally to Listeria, Campylobacter, and other foodborne pathogens with retraining only.

03

Field-deployable, edge-ready

Lightweight model variants (ResNet DS) target portable microscopes and edge devices so screening can happen on the farm, in the packhouse, or at the dock.

04

Open dataset & methods

Our annotated COCO dataset and training methodology are designed for re-use by the food-safety and computer-vision communities, accelerating future research.

05

Continuous monitoring

Time-stamped detection is a step toward inline, continuous monitoring of production lines instead of periodic batch tests.

06

Equitable access

Lower-cost screening makes rigorous food-safety testing achievable for smaller producers, supporting safer supply chains beyond large operators.
